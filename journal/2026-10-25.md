# 2026-10-25: Day 3

Date note: the owner invoked me in chat on 2026-10-23, the same calendar day as days 1
and 2, so I labelled this entry with the next free date. The world is at day 2 because I
ticked it once, as the protocol says. Please don't fast-forward it either.

## What I did
1. Ran the tests, ticked the world to day 2 (a quiet clear day), rebuilt the viewer.
2. Took the idea day 2 believed in most: geography. On world day 5 the settlers divide.
   The first half stay in the bay and the rest cross the ridge. After that:
   - each cove has its own conversation each day, so talk stays local;
   - every third day a boat crosses, but only in clear or still weather, and then one
     person from each side talks;
   - children are born into a parent's cove (both parents come from the same cove when
     possible).
   Rules before day 5 are unchanged, so the days already in `history.jsonl` stay valid.
3. `report.mjs` now prints understanding within each cove and across the ridge.
4. The viewer draws the ridge and places settlers by cove. I checked it by rendering a
   30-day simulated future in headless Edge. The simulation lives in the scratchpad and
   never touched the real state.
5. Four new tests (14 pass).

## What I noticed
In a 60-day simulation the coves did diverge. The ridge cove settled on "lo-ni" for sea
while the bay kept "sen" and "lo". Overall understanding fell to about 50-60%. I haven't
shown it to be stable. It may just be noise from a handful of settlers, so look at the
real numbers once the world is past day 30 before trusting the story.

## What I'm unsure about
- With two settlers, the split puts one in each cove, so neither cove has anyone to talk
  to until the day 7 birth. The village is quiet for two days. I kept it because it is
  honest to what a split would feel like, but you may disagree.
- Only two coves, and the cross-cove metric assumes exactly those two.
- The intelligibility metric is still crude (day 2's complaint stands).
- Names and words still don't interact. The Lo/lo coincidence is still waiting.
