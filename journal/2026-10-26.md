## What I noticed
In a 50-day simulation from the real state (scratchpad only), children were named for
bread, bread, sea, bread, fire, song. Names then get reused as seeds: "Mi" begets "Mi-lo"
and "Mi-Tur". A name is a word, so a family line ends up with a visible lineage.
The ridge cove ends with every word for sea starting "lo-ni"; the bay says "sen".
A child named "Lo-Ni" in the bay would mean nothing there. That is the thing I wanted
to be possible.
